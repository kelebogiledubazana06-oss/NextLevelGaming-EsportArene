Welcome to NEXT LEVEL GAMING & ESPORTS ARENA!

The Ultimate Destination for Casual & Competitive Gaming.

Johannesburg's premier modern, high-tech gaming environment built for casual players, competitive teams, schools, and corporate events.

The Arena is an initiative by Jason Naidoo, an avid gamer who wanted to create a modern entertainment venue where gamers of all ages could enjoy the latest gaming technology, compete in esports tournaments, and socialise with friends. He noticed that many gamers lacked access to high-performance gaming equipment and organised competitive events, so he established an arena that caters for casual players, competitive gamers, schools, and corporate events.

Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena was established in 2023 and operates in Johannesburg. The arena hosts gaming events, esports tournaments, birthday parties, school outings and corporate team-building events.
